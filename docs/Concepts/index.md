* [Agent Definition](agent-definition.md) - An AI agent is an autonomous software entity that can perceive its environment, make decisions, and take actions to achieve specific goals
* [Agent Foundational](agent-foundational.md) - Foundational definitions, internal architecture, and core capabilities of AI agents drawn from primary academic and industry sources.
* [Agent Types](agent-types.md) - AI agents can be classified based on various characteristics including their capabilities, architecture, and application domains
* [AI Engineering](ai-engineering.md) - AI engineering is the process of building applications on top of readily available foundation models
* [2026 AI Predictions (You.com Whitepaper)](ai-predictions-2026.md) - The central thesis is a shift from **Chat-Engines** (AI systems you converse with) to **Do-Engines** (AI systems that autonomously complete tasks end-to-end)
* [Foundation Models](foundation-models.md) - Foundation models are large-scale AI models trained on broad data that can be adapted to a wide range of downstream tasks
